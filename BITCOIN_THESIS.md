## Purpose of this document

This is not investment advice. This is a personal commitment device.

The goal: when emotion takes over (fear in bear markets, FOMO in bull markets), have a written framework I trust more than my impulses in that moment. The thesis below is what *past, calm RodSan* tells *present, panicking RodSan* to do.

Every claim here must be defensible by data I understand. If I can't explain a rule to a non-Bitcoin friend, the rule doesn't belong here.

---

## Personal context

- **Investment horizon**: 4+ years (full market cycles)
- **Risk tolerance**: Tolerates large drawdowns in exchange for long-term asymmetric upside
- **Goal**: Accumulate during accumulation zones, reduce exposure during distribution zones, hold through everything in between
- **What I am NOT trying to do**: time exact bottoms or tops, day-trade, predict short-term price action

---

## Core beliefs (working hypotheses)

*These are starting positions, not conclusions. To be challenged and refined as I learn.*

1. **Bitcoin operates in roughly 4-year cycles**, historically anchored to halving events. Whether this pattern persists is an open question, but until proven otherwise, it's the working model.

2. **On-chain metrics describe holder behavior, not future prices.** They tell me where the market is in its cycle, not what price comes next.

3. **Patient capital outperforms reactive capital** over long horizons. The goal is to act like a long-term holder, not a trader.

4. **Multiple confirming signals beat a single signal.** No single metric is reliable on its own. Confluence across metric families is what matters.

5. **The cost of being early is small; the cost of being late can be large.** Accumulation during clear undervaluation, even if not at the absolute bottom, captures most of the asymmetric upside.

🚧 *Iteration 1 — in construction*

**What it tells me**: The aggregate cost basis of the market. Whether the average holder is in profit or in paper loss.

**My signal interpretation**:
- Market price **far above** Realized Price → market is in profit, mid-to-late cycle
- Market price **at** Realized Price → equilibrium, neutral
- Market price **below** Realized Price → aggregate paper loss, historically rare, historically generational

### MVRV Z-Score

🚧 *Iteration 1 — in construction*

**My signal interpretation**:
- Z < 0.1 → extreme undervaluation, historically generational buy zones
- 0.1 to 2.5 → fair value
- 2.5 to 5 → market stretching, caution warranted
- Z > 5 → extreme overvaluation, historically distribution zones

## Confluence framework (Iteration 5)

🔲 *Will be completed when all individual metrics have been observed in real time and their personal thresholds calibrated.*

The goal of this section: a clear decision matrix that combines signals from all 6 metric families into a single qualitative answer to the question:

> "Given everything the on-chain data is showing, where am I in the cycle, and what is the appropriate action?"

Possible outputs:
- **Strong accumulate**: multiple metric families in extreme undervaluation
- **Accumulate**: one metric family in extreme undervaluation, others neutral or supportive
- **Hold**: no extreme readings, no clear signal
- **Caution**: one or more metric families in extreme overvaluation
- **Reduce**: multiple metric families in extreme overvaluation

## Anti-patterns (things I commit to NOT do)

*Things I might be tempted to do that this framework explicitly discourages.*

- ❌ Sell during cycle accumulation zones because price is "still going down"
- ❌ Buy during cycle distribution zones because price is "still going up"
- ❌ Trust a single metric without confirmation from other metric families
- ❌ Treat the `btc-cycle` tool as predictive rather than descriptive
- ❌ Look at metrics more than once per day (they update daily — there's nothing new to see in between)
- ❌ Add new metrics to the tool faster than I can internalize them
- ❌ Change the thesis based on price action — only based on new evidence or new understanding
